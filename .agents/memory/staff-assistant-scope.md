name: Staff assistant scope
description: Audience, human approval, and business-knowledge boundaries required for IPM Assistant.
---

IPM Assistant is initially an internal staff tool. Direct owner access and automatic sending to clients are future scope, not authorized by the initial request. Staff review and copy the generated reply themselves.

**Why:** The user explicitly prioritized staff drafting and requested that the existing public chatbot remain intact.

**How to apply:** Keep new assistant functions behind staff authorization. Ask before adding a customer-facing assistant or enabling automatic sending.

The listing-promotion terms supplied for the staff assistant are its authoritative business source. Do not silently replace the public chatbot's older program knowledge with those terms or merge the two sources.

**Why:** The user requested reuse of the AI architecture, not a public-chatbot policy rewrite; the audiences and approved program descriptions differ.

**How to apply:** Treat public and internal business-knowledge changes separately and preserve the public embed unless the user explicitly requests its modification.
